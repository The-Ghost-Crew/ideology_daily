How this works

An ideology is filed as the mystery answer. Type any ideology you know into the box — a dropdown will narrow it down as you type — and press Guess. You'll get back an entry card showing how that ideology's own compass zone(s) and category tags line up against the one that's actually filed. Identify the filed ideology in 15 entries or fewer.

The number on a tag is its rarity score, computed fresh from whatever's currently on file — log-weighted by how few entries carry it. Higher number, rarer tag, bigger boost when it's shared. These examples update automatically as entries are added, so they can't go stale.

The stamp blends zone overlap (40% of the score) with rarity-weighted category overlap (60%) — matching one rare tag can outweigh matching several common ones.

Fictional ideologies

A few entries are marked Fictional — no real-world basis, usually pulled from fiction (like IngSoc from Nineteen Eighty-Four). Guess one and you'll get a badge: green if the answer is fictional too, red if it isn't. It only shows up when the entry you guessed is itself fictional — guessing a real-world entry reveals nothing on this axis.

Ismism

"Ismism believes that any and all combination of philosophical and political thought could be transformed into an ideology, regardless of the seriousness or consistency of the statements in question."

Ismism is a wildcard guess, always available — and yes, it can occasionally be the actual filed answer too, since it's built to represent every zone and every tag at once. Guess it against a different answer and it reveals a random slice of zones and tags checked against the real one instead, plus sometimes whether it's fictional. How much it shows depends on timing and a little luck: early in the game it's barely a sliver; late in the game it can lay out almost everything — in Easy, it ramps up faster and is basically maxed out by entry 12. There's a dedicated button for it next to the guess form.

A couple of extras

Guessing the same ideology twice won't burn an entry — it points you back to the card instead. The Confirmed box below collects every zone and tag a guess has proven true about the answer, so you don't have to scroll back through every card to remember.

Daily puzzle

There are two separate daily puzzles, Normal and Easy — pick with the tabs once you're in, like difficulty tiers in a daily crossword. Easy shows the answer's compass zones upfront as its own hint, separate from Confirmed and Candidates — those two still only reflect what your actual guesses have proven, so they correctly start empty even in Easy. Normal gets no hint. Each track has its own puzzle number and its own stats. Nothing locks you out after solving either — "New ideology" gives unlimited random practice any time, or head back to a daily whenever you like. Solve a daily and you can share your result as a row of colored squares (🟩🟨🟥, one per guess) without spoiling the answer. Stats and in-progress games save locally in your browser and survive a refresh, same as Wordle.

Candidates

The Candidates box lists every ideology that could still be the answer given what's confirmed so far — it narrows on its own as you guess, nothing to tap. Full spoiler if you open it early, so it's entirely optional.
